What the environment represents
Robot and wrist assembly
Articulated Lite 6 meshes, vacuum tool, approximate rigid OAK camera bracket and view frustum. The geometry starts from your existing GitHub project's simulator assets.
Measurement area
Stationary target and distractor on a plane, a calibration/checkerboard artifact and an independently positioned reference camera. The lab arrangement is illustrative.
Supporting equipment
Table, lighting, host/recording workstation, network/PoE representation and physical E-stop representation. Rendered equipment does not establish real electrical compatibility or readiness.
