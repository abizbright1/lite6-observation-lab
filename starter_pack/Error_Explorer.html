When does the possible target fit the task?

A static target is seen by a translating camera. An old image is combined with the current camera position. The apparent target is fixed at 102 mm and the proposed tool position at 100 mm.

X = 102 − velocity × age − bias − noise. Age has a fixed lower bound of 20 ms. Noise is assumed within ±0.1 mm.

Blue segment: possible target positions. Green area: acceptable region around 100 mm. Orange point: apparent estimate 102 mm.

Teaching values only. This is ordinary arithmetic for an assumed scalar model, not a robot controller, physical safety certificate or validation of the assumptions. Changing the tolerance here does not justify changing a laboratory limit.

Try a 2 mm tolerance, then set velocity to zero. Explain why the result changes, and what calibration uncertainty remains. An old image of a stationary scene is not automatically invalid.
