Your 16 week weekday research plan

Updated 3 November 2026 to your explicit correction: 3–4 hours every day, Monday through Friday. Saturday and Sunday are completely off. This replaces the earlier mistaken weekly-total assumption. Reserve 15–20 hours each week, or 240–320 hours over 16 weeks. This is a foundation, reproducibility and research-feasibility program, not a promise of a finished dissertation, unique contribution or independent robot-operating readiness.

Use the additional time for derivations, coding, repeated comparisons and explained outputs. The conditional topic remains positioning decisions under coupled camera timing and geometric-calibration uncertainty. More available time does not replace human-supervisor fit, a strong full-joint baseline, credible metrology or a useful physical regime.

Daily structure: Monday–Friday only

Each weekday has a 180-minute core, with an optional 60-minute extension on that same weekday to reach four hours. Breaks are inside the core. No additional homework, Saturday work or Sunday catch-up is assumed.

The core contains 150 focused minutes and 30 break minutes per day. Five core days reserve 900 minutes = 15 hours, including 12.5 focused hours and 2.5 break hours. All five extensions add five focused hours, giving 20 reserved hours weekly. Sixteen weeks reserve 240–320 hours: 200–280 focused hours plus 40 break hours. Friday's adviser discussion fits the final 30-minute core block. Saturday: 0 planned minutes. Sunday: 0 planned minutes.

Each day below fills these blocks. If an exercise finishes early, test another boundary case, explain it without notes and improve the same artifact. If it takes longer, use the next weekday or extend the calendar; do not use a weekend or silently exceed the chosen daily limit. Optional work is not necessary for a core pass.

Progression and limits

An adviser may accept demonstrated prerequisites and bring a topic forward; code execution alone is insufficient. Mark every Friday ready, needs practice or needs help. Repeat weak prerequisites during later weekdays, extending the calendar if necessary. Access work, troubleshooting and logging all count inside the daily budget.

At 15–20 reserved hours weekly, substantially more practice and investigation are possible than under the superseded weekly-total assumption. A doctoral finish date still requires approved scope, an original useful result, accessible apparatus/reference and human-supervisor milestones. Week 16 is a research-stage decision. Keep negative results: if a fresh settled image already solves the practical issue or existing joint methods meet the deadline, narrow or change direction. No expensive purchase or robot motion is required by this foundation plan.
